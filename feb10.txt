1.Google Maps predicts traffic because machine learning studies past travel times, live GPS data, and patterns to guess how crowded roads will be.

2.YouTube recommendations work since ML watches what you click, how long you watch, and compares it with millions of others to predict what you’ll like next.

3.Email spam filters learn from examples of spam vs real emails and keep adjusting their rules based on what people mark as junk.

4.Voice assistants like Siri or Google Assistant use ML to convert speech into text and then predict the most likely meaning of what you said.

5.Netflix suggestions happen because ML analyzes your watch history and compares it with viewers who have similar tastes.

6.Online shopping sites recommend products by using ML to study what you browsed, bought, or even paused on.

7.Phone face unlock works because ML models learn facial patterns and match them with stored features of your face.

8.Instagram or Facebook feeds are arranged by ML systems that predict which posts you’re most likely to engage with.

9.Keyboard autocorrect and next-word prediction rely on language models trained on massive text data to guess what you intend to type.

10.Banking fraud detection uses ML to notice unusual spending patterns compared to your normal habits and flags suspicious activity.
