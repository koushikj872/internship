College:
- Student Performance Prediction: Attendance, study hours, assignment scores → Final exam grade
- Course Recommendation System: Student interests, completed courses, grades → Recommended courses
- Student Dropout Prediction: Attendance, academic records, engagement data → Dropout risk (High/Medium/Low)
- Placement Prediction: CGPA, skills, projects, internships → Placement probability

Healthcare:
- Disease Prediction: Symptoms, age, medical history → Predicted disease
- Hospital Readmission Prediction: Patient records, treatment history → Readmission risk
- Heart Disease Detection: Blood pressure, cholesterol, ECG data → Heart disease likelihood
- Medical Appointment No-Show Prediction: Appointment history, demographics → Will attend or not

Shopping:
- Product Recommendation: Browsing history, purchases, ratings → Recommended products
- Customer Churn Prediction: Purchase frequency, spending habits → Churn risk
- Demand Forecasting: Historical sales, season, promotions → Future product demand
- Review Sentiment Analysis: Customer reviews → Positive/Negative/Neutral sentiment
